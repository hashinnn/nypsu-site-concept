Featured Highlights

Freshman Orientation

The Freshman Orientation warmly welcomes new students annually by introducing them to campus facilities, student services, and a series of fun, interactive activities designed to help freshmen feel connected and ready to thrive in their new academic journey.

Student Life Carnival

The Student Life Carnival is an exciting annual event celebrating the vibrant student community at NYP. It features a wide range of performances, fun activities, and showcases from various student clubs and organizations to foster spirit, friendship, and creativity on campus.

Graduation Night

Graduation Night is a grand celebration honoring the achievements of graduating students. It features an elegant dinner, speeches by distinguished guests, and entertainment that marks the culmination of academic success and the start of new adventures.
